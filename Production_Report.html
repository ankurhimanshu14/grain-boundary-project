CQI-9 · production record and traceability

Production report

Load trays

Enter the trays in the order they enter the hardening chamber. Each tray enters one push time after the one before it. Give the loading details once and the number of trays with the same loading; use “Add to the last tray” for a mixed tray and “Add empty tray(s)” for trays without material.

Tray log

One line per tray. Select trays to put them on hold, release them, mark them as rework or delete them; “Edit” changes the contents, an actual entry time (a tray that entered off the cycle, e.g. after a stop) or the note.

Process parameter changes

Every change made during the day: push time (it changes the timing of the trays from that moment: enter the time of the first push at the new push time), set values per zone, controlled cooling time, quench oil temperature or anything else. A set value or cooling time that differs from the specification of a batch in that zone is flagged on the batch.

Trays on hold

Trays held because of an issue (temperature excursion, alarm, parameter out of specification, test pending …) in this furnace, with their release: released after a check, sent for rework, scrapped or released under concession.

Batches

Built from the tray log: one entry per part, batch, heat and heat code (rework separately). Trays, pieces, weight, entry, exit, push time and zone windows come from the trays.

Totals of the day

Per part, per heat and per process for the furnace and date chosen above; rework is counted separately from first-time production.

Approval

When every tray and batch is complete, the supervisor approves the report. It is then locked; unlocking needs a reason and is recorded. Holds can still be released on a locked report.

Process specifications

One specification per part no.; it is the same in every furnace. Set values per zone of the hardening and tempering chamber, push time, cooling times, loading per tray and part weight.

Search by heat no. or batch no.

Every batch of every day and furnace that matches.

Settings and data

Tray positions per zone are written as three numbers, e.g. 5,4,4 (Zone 1, 2, 3). Times between the chambers: hardening & tempering = quench delay + dip time + transfer to the tempering chamber; isothermal annealing = the controlled cooling cycle: transfer into the controlled cooling chamber + the part's controlled cooling time + transfer into the tempering chamber (the transfers are set here), all within the same push cycle; normalizing and full annealing = the values set here.

How the times are worked out
  • The trays are entered in the order they enter the hardening chamber. Tray n + 1 enters one push time after tray n; a push time change takes effect at its time (the first push at the new push time); a tray with an actual entry time starts the cycle again from that time.
  • The line: hardening chamber 13 tray positions (5 + 4 + 4), tempering chamber 12 (4 + 4 + 4). At every push the extractor takes the 13th tray out of the hardening chamber and a new tray is pushed in; at the same time a tray is taken out of the tempering chamber. For isothermal annealing the extracted tray goes into the controlled cooling chamber (one tray), air is blown for the part's controlled cooling time, and the tray is then pushed into the tempering chamber, all before the next push.
  • Measured on this line: first tray in at 01:06, out of the hardening chamber at 05:00 (13 pushes of 18 min) and out of the tempering chamber at 08:18 (11 more pushes). A tray therefore leaves the hardening chamber 13 pushes after it entered and the tempering chamber 11 pushes later (setting “pushes until a tray is taken out”). The minutes between the chambers delay the entry into the tempering chamber, not the exit.
  • A tray moves on when the next trays are pushed in, so the trays entered on the next day (or, when there are none yet, the push time in force) give the time each tray leaves a zone and the furnace. Zone windows of a batch: from the arrival of its first tray to the departure of its last tray in each zone; they are the basis for tracing batches in a temperature excursion.
  • A batch is one part no. with one batch no., heat no. and heat code; trays of a rework (re-heat-treatment) are a separate entry. When the batch no. is not available (N/A), trays in a row with the same details form one entry.
