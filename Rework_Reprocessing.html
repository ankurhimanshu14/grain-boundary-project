1.11 Does the heat treater have a documented procedure for the rework / reprocessing of parts?

Rework / reprocessing of heat treated components can have a significant impact on the performance of the component. Reworking / reprocessing in some cases is an acceptable practice. A rework / reprocessing procedure is key to identifying the rework / reprocessing practice. To be approved for rework / reprocessing, either on a case basis or pre-approved in the PPAP, the heat treater shall meet the following requirements.

  • Any change or addition to the rework / reprocessing procedure shall require notification and approval in accordance with the AIAG PPAP process. Any rework / reprocessing not previously approved and identified in the rework / reprocessing procedure is not allowed.
  • The OEM shall be notified by the Tier 1 supplier prior to rework / reprocessing product utilizing an unapproved process. If not Tier 1, the customer shall be notified.
  • The rework / reprocessing procedure shall be referenced in the heat treater's PPAP approved PFMEA and process control plan.
  • The procedure shall include: a description of product characteristics for which rework / reprocessing is allowed and those for which it is not permissible; a requirement that all rework / reprocessing activity have a new process control sheet issued by qualified personnel, including the heat treat parameter modification; a requirement that there is a record or log of all rework / reprocessing work; a description of the sampling plan; a requirement that the Quality Manager or a designee shall authorize the release of rework / reprocessed product.

Method
  • Procedure: one controlled rework / reprocessing procedure: the characteristics that may and may not be reworked, the approved methods (each marked when pre-approved in the PPAP, with a limit on how often a lot may be reworked), the process control sheet, the log, the sampling plan, the release and the customer notification. Approved revisions are locked; a change starts a new revision that must be notified and approved through PPAP before use.
  • Each rework job: a characteristic not permissible for rework cannot be reworked (scrap, return or customer concession). A method not in the procedure or not pre-approved in the PPAP, a case-basis rework, or a lot past the method's limit needs the OEM / customer notification and approval before the rework starts.
  • Process control sheet: issued by qualified personnel before the rework, with the heat treat parameter modification.
  • Sampling and release: the sampling plan of the procedure; release only by the Quality Manager or a designee after a passing result.
  • Log: every rework tray on the daily production reports must have its job in this log (matched by the rework reference, or by part and batch).
