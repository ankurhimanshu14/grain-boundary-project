Failed surveys: reaction plans

A failed survey disqualifies the range at that temperature. Product processed in the chamber since the last passing survey at that temperature is at risk; the reaction closes only with root cause, correction, product-impact assessment and a passing re-survey.

Settings, qualified ranges and data

Qualified operating ranges per chamber

A chamber used for several processes is qualified over the whole span it runs at (e.g. the hardening chamber preheats for normalizing and austenitizes for hardening). Tolerance and interval come from the furnace class and instrumentation type: confirm them against the controlled CQI-9 copy (pyrometry section and the applicable process table).

Data

Records are kept in this browser only. Export a backup regularly and import it on another computer.

Method
  • CQI-9 requires a TUS of every furnace work zone at the extremes of each qualified operating range, initially and then periodically (the interval depends on the furnace class and instrumentation type), and after changes that can affect uniformity (rebuild, burner or control-thermocouple relocation, a failed survey).
  • Every TUS thermocouple reading is corrected with its correction factor. After the furnace has stabilised, every corrected reading must lie within set value ± tolerance; readings before stabilisation are shown but not judged. A survey with a reading beyond the warning margin (default 80 % of the tolerance) passes but is flagged.
  • A survey is invalid when the test instrument or the TUS thermocouples were out of calibration on the survey day.
  • The tolerance, interval, number of TUS thermocouples and readings here are editable defaults: confirm them against the controlled CQI-9 copy, the furnace class of each chamber and the customer requirements.
