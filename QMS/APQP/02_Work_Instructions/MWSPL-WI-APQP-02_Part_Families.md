### 1. When a family may be used

Parts may share one feasibility study when all of the following are the same or within a stated range:

| Criterion | Example |
|---|---|
| Process route and furnace | Isothermal annealing on F-03 |
| Material group | 20MnCr5 / 16MnCr5 |
| Section and weight range | 30–60 mm, 2–5 kg |
| Requirements | Hardness 140–180 HB, no other test |
| Loading | Same tray type and load build principle |

A part with its own special characteristic, a different quench or a requirement outside the family's range gets its own study.

### 2. Define the family

| Step | Action | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Application → Quality planning → "New study (part family)": family name, definition (the criteria of §1 with their ranges) and the parts, one per line. | HT Manager |
| 2.2 | Complete the study as WI-APQP-01, answering the questions for the most difficult part of the family (largest section, heaviest load). | Team |
| 2.3 | Approve it. Every part named is then covered (Parts coverage). The register F-APQP-03 is printed from the HTSA 1.2 evidence. | HT Manager |

### 3. Adding a part

Check the part against the definition. If it fits, start a new revision of the family study, add the part, give the reason and approve it again. If it does not fit, give it its own study.
