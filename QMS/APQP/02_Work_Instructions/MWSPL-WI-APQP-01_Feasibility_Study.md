# MASS WIRE AND STEELS PVT. LTD., BHIWADI

## FEASIBILITY STUDY OF A PART

| Doc No. | Rev | Issue date | Process Owner | Prepared | Approved |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MWSPL-WI-APQP-01 | 00 | — | Quality Manager | | |

### 1. Start the study

| Step | Action | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Application → Quality planning → "New study (part)", or Parts coverage → "Start study" for a part with a specification in force. One study per part; a change is a new revision of it. | Quality Manager |
| 1.2 | Enter part number and name, drawing and revision, customer, material grade, process, the furnace(s) proposed, the reason for planning and the target date. | Quality Manager |

### 2. Customer requirements

| Step | Action | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Review the customer-specific requirements and the customer's heat treat specification; record the document and revision, who reviewed and the date. | Quality Manager |
| 2.2 | Note the requirements that drive the planning: special characteristics, testing and sampling, packaging, PPAP level. | Quality Manager |

### 3. Feasibility questions (F-APQP-01)

| Step | Action | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Enter volume, pieces per tray, part weight and push time: the application shows the furnace hours a month and the net load per tray. | Production Manager |
| 3.2 | Answer every question Yes, No or Not applicable; a No or Not applicable needs a note. The application shows hints: furnaces that do not run the process, the quench tank of each furnace, tray load, capacity. | Team |
| 3.3 | Conclude: feasible as specified; feasible with the changes listed (list them); or not feasible (give the reason). "Feasible as specified" is not possible with a question answered No. | Team |

### 4. Approval

| Step | Action | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | Enter the team (at least two, name and function), prepared by and approved by. Only an authorized person may approve (HT organization: heat treat person, deputy, authorized positions; or the names in Settings). | Quality Manager |
| 4.2 | "Approve feasibility": the application refuses while anything is missing and lists it. Approved = locked. Print F-APQP-01 and sign it. | HT Manager |
| 4.3 | A later change (drawing, material, requirement, furnace, family member): "New revision"; the approved revision stays in force until the new one is approved. | Quality Manager |

### 5. Planning outputs and release (F-APQP-02)

| Step | Action | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | For each output (process flow, PFMEA, control plan, process specification, loading standard, trial lot, PPAP) record Done with its reference and date, or Not applicable with the reason. The process specification in force is shown from the Specifications module. | Quality Manager |
| 5.2 | When feasible only with changes, tick that the changes are made. | HT Manager |
| 5.3 | Enter released by and the date and press "Release". Production may run the part from then on. | Quality Manager |
