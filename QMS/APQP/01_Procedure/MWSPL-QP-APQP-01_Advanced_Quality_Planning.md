### 1. Purpose

To plan every part before it is heat treated in production: review the customer's requirements, study and internally approve the feasibility of each part (alone or as part of a defined part family), and complete the planning outputs before the part is released (CQI-9 HTSA 1.2).

### 2. Scope

All parts heat treated on furnaces F-01 to F-04 (hardening & tempering, isothermal annealing, normalizing): new parts, changes of drawing, material or customer requirement, a change of process or furnace, and parts transferred from another heat treater.

### 3. References

- AIAG CQI-9 4th Edition, HTSA 1.2 (controlled copy); HTSA 1.3 (PFMEA), 1.4 (control plan), 1.6 (process specifications).
- IATF 16949:2016 §8.2.3, §8.3; AIAG APQP manual (context).
- MWSPL-WI-APQP-01, -02; MWSPL-F-APQP-01, -02, -03; MWSPL-QP-SPEC-01; MWSPL-QP-ORG-01.

### 4. Terms and definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Feasibility study | The team's review of whether the part can be heat treated to all requirements on the proposed furnace(s), recorded on F-APQP-01 with a conclusion: feasible as specified, feasible with the changes listed, or not feasible. |
| Part family | Parts the heat treater groups because they share the process route and furnace, material group, section and weight range and requirements, so one feasibility study covers them all (WI-APQP-02). |
| Planning outputs | Process flow, PFMEA, control plan, process specification, loading standard, trial lot with results, PPAP / customer approval (F-APQP-02). |
| Release | The Quality Manager's decision that the planning is complete and the part may be run in production. |

### 6. Procedure

#### 6.1 Trigger

A planning record is started for every new part, every change of drawing, material, customer requirement, process or furnace, and every transferred part. Production does not run a part without an approved feasibility study.

#### 6.2 Feasibility study (WI-APQP-01)

1. The team (at least two functions) reviews the drawing, material and every heat treat requirement and the customer-specific requirements.
2. It answers the feasibility questions of F-APQP-01: part definition, customer requirements, material, furnace and process, qualified temperature range, load build and tray load, quench or controlled cooling, testing, capacity, special characteristics, shot blasting / handling / traceability.
3. It concludes: feasible as specified, feasible with the changes listed, or not feasible (with the reason).
4. The study is internally approved by a person authorized in the HT organization (the heat treat person, the deputy, or a position marked authorized). Approved = locked; a change is a new revision.

#### 6.3 Part families (WI-APQP-02)

Similar parts may share one study. The family is defined in writing; a part added to an approved family needs a new revision of the family study.

#### 6.4 Planning outputs and release

After the approval the outputs of F-APQP-02 are completed: process flow, PFMEA (HTSA 1.3), control plan (HTSA 1.4), process specification (HTSA 1.6), loading standard, trial lot and results, PPAP / customer approval. Where the study was feasible only with changes, the changes are confirmed. The Quality Manager then releases the part. An output that does not apply is marked so with the reason.

#### 6.5 Not feasible

The customer is informed; the part is not run until a new study (other process / furnace / agreed deviation) concludes feasible. A cancelled planning stays on record.

### 7. Records

| Record | Kept as | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| Team feasibility commitment | F-APQP-01 (application, printed and signed) | Life of the part + 1 year |
| Quality planning checklist and release | F-APQP-02 | Life of the part + 1 year |

### 8. Review

This procedure is reviewed every 365 days and after a customer complaint traced to planning; a review is recorded as a new revision.
