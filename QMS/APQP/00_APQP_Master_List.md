# MASS WIRE AND STEELS PVT. LTD., BHIWADI

## APQP MODULE: ADVANCED QUALITY PLANNING — MASTER LIST

**Basis:** AIAG CQI-9 4th Edition, HTSA question 1.2 ("Does the heat treater perform advanced quality planning?"): the heat treater has a documented advanced quality planning procedure; a feasibility study is performed and internally approved for each part; similar parts can be grouped into part families for this effort, as defined by the heat treater (wording to confirm against the controlled copy). IATF 16949 §8.2.3 (review of the requirements, feasibility) and §8.3 (product and process design, APQP) as context. PDCA: **Plan** (before a part is run).

**Application:** CALOR Quality System (`QMS/00_Integrated_Application/calor-quality-system.html`) → Quality planning. The approval of the procedure, the feasibility studies (single parts and part families), the planning outputs, the release for production, the parts coverage and the HTSA 1.2 self-assessment are held there.

### Where each requirement is met

| Requirement (HTSA 1.2) | Met by | Evidence in the application |
|---|---|---|
| A documented advanced quality planning procedure | QP-APQP-01 | Procedure approval recorded (number, revision, approved by, date); periodic review |
| A feasibility study for each part | QP-APQP-01 §6.2; WI-APQP-01; F-APQP-01 | Parts coverage: every part with a specification in force has a study |
| Internally approved | WI-APQP-01 §4 | Approval gate: questions answered, conclusion, team, approved by an authorized person |
| Part families as defined by the heat treater | WI-APQP-02; F-APQP-03 | Family studies with a definition and the parts covered |
| Customer requirements incorporated | WI-APQP-01 §2 | Customer-specific requirements reviewed (reference, by, date) in every study |
| Planning completed before production | QP-APQP-01 §6.4; F-APQP-02 | Planning outputs (flow, PFMEA, control plan, specification, load build, trial, PPAP) and release |

### First-cycle implementation checklist

- [ ] Approve QP-APQP-01, the two WIs and the formats; record the approval in the application (Procedure & part families).
- [ ] Set who may approve a feasibility study (Settings, or the HT organization module) and the processes run on each furnace.
- [ ] Define the part families (WI-APQP-02) for the parts already in production.
- [ ] Record a feasibility study for every part or family with a process specification in force (Parts coverage → Start study).
- [ ] Complete the planning outputs and release each part; check that HTSA 1.2 reads Satisfactory; file the evidence.
