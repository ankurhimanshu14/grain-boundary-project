# MASS WIRE AND STEELS PVT. LTD., BHIWADI

## PROTECTION TUBE CHECK AND REPLACEMENT

| Doc No. | Rev | Issue date | Used by | Prepared | Approved |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MWSPL-WI-TC-03 | 00 | — | Maintenance technician | | |

**Plant rule:** the element and its protection tube form one assembly; they are installed and replaced together. A tube is never replaced on its own: replacing the tube means replacing the thermocouple (element and tube, WI-TC-01 §2) and running a SAT before product.

### 1. When

At the check interval of each thermocouple in the approved PM schedule (Application → PM schedule; default every 30 days), at every element replacement and at every furnace shutdown that opens the chamber.

### 2. Check

| Step | Action | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Application → Tube checks → "Tube check round"; enter the date and your name. | Technician |
| 2.2 | For each tube due: look at the visible part and the head; at shutdown, the part inside the chamber. | Technician |
| 2.3 | Result **OK**: no scaling beyond light oxide, no bending, no cracks, head tight. | Technician |
| 2.4 | Result **Worn / scaled**: heavy scaling, wall loss, bending. Write what you see. Replace the thermocouple (element and tube) before the next check. | Technician |
| 2.5 | Result **Cracked / burnt through**: replace the thermocouple (element and tube) at once, then a SAT before product (WI-TC-02). | Technician |

### 3. Replacement

| Step | Action | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Replace the thermocouple: new element and new tube of the scheduled material (or the approved alternative) together. Record it in Application → Thermocouple replacements (reason "Protection tube failure" for a worn / cracked tube, service life for the tube life); enter the tube material fitted. The new assembly's check interval and tube life start on the replacement date. | Technician |
| 3.2 | Run a SAT of the new element before product (WI-TC-02). | Technician |
| 3.3 | A change of material or interval goes into the PM schedule; the Maintenance Head re-approves it. | Maintenance Head |
