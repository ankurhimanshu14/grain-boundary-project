## HEAT TREAT ORGANIZATION AND QUALIFICATION OF THE HEAT TREAT PERSON

### 1. Purpose

To make sure the heat treatment of the plant is in the charge of a dedicated, qualified, full-time heat treat person on site, shown in the organization chart, with a job description that states the qualifications for the position (CQI-9 HTSA 1.1).

### 2. Scope

The heat treat organization of the Bhiwadi plant: furnaces F-01 to F-04 and the functions around them (production, quality, maintenance, laboratory). The positions of other departments are recorded as far as they appear in the reporting lines.

### 3. References

- AIAG CQI-9 4th Edition, HTSA 1.1 (controlled copy); HTSA 1.6 (authorization to approve process specifications).
- IATF 16949:2016 §5.3, §7.2.
- MWSPL-WI-ORG-01, -02; MWSPL-F-ORG-01, -02, -03; MWSPL-QP-SPEC-01.

### 4. Terms and definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Heat treat person | The holder of the one position marked "heat treat position" (HT Manager): responsible for the heat treat process and its metallurgical results. |
| Dedicated | Heat treatment is the main duty of the position, and its holder holds no other position. |
| Heat treat experience | Months of heat treat work from dated experience entries plus the service in this organization since joining, when that is heat treat work; overlapping periods count once. |
| Metallurgical education | The years of education in metallurgy (degree or diploma courses marked metallurgical). |
| Qualified (HTSA 1.1) | Heat treat experience ≥ 5 years, or heat treat experience and metallurgical education together ≥ 5 years, both above zero. |

### 5. Responsibility and authority

| Role | Responsibility | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Director | Appoints the heat treat person and the deputy; approves the organization chart; owns this procedure | Appoint, approve |
| HR executive | Keeps the qualification records and job descriptions; prepares job descriptions | — |
| HT Manager (heat treat person) | Runs the heat treat process; approves process specifications (HTSA 1.6); keeps the qualification record up to date | Stop the line, hold and release product, approve specifications |
| Deputy | Covers absences of the heat treat person | As delegated |

### 6. Procedure

#### 6.1 The heat treat position

1. One position is marked as the heat treat position. It is dedicated, full-time and on site, and it reports to a position in the organization chart.
2. Its holder meets the qualification of §4 and the job description; a deputy is named to cover absences.
3. A vacancy or a holder below the minimum is a "needs immediate action" finding: the Director appoints a qualified person (WI-ORG-01).

#### 6.2 Organization chart and job descriptions

1. The chart is drawn from the reporting lines of the positions and approved by the Director (F-ORG-03). A change of a title, reporting line, holder, deputy, heat treat flag, full-time, location or dedication, or a position added or removed, needs a new approval. The chart is reviewed every 365 days.
2. Every position has a job description (F-ORG-01) under document control: approved = locked; a change is a new revision, the approved one stays in force until the new one is approved. The job description of the heat treat position states the metallurgical and heat treat knowledge and at least 5 years of experience.

#### 6.3 Authorization to approve process specifications

The holder and deputy of the heat treat position, and of positions marked "authorized to approve process specifications", may approve process specifications (MWSPL-QP-SPEC-01). The application gives this list to the Specifications module's approval gate.
