# MASS WIRE AND STEELS PVT. LTD., BHIWADI

## ORG MODULE: HEAT TREAT ORGANIZATION — MASTER LIST

**Basis:** AIAG CQI-9 4th Edition, HTSA question 1.1 ("Is there a dedicated and qualified heat treat person on-site?"): the heat treat person is dedicated, qualified, full-time and on site; the position is shown in the organization chart; a job description identifies the qualifications for the position, including metallurgical and heat treat knowledge; the person has evidence of at least 5 years of heat treat experience or a combination of formal metallurgical education and heat treat experience (minimum to confirm against the controlled copy). IATF 16949 §5.3 (roles and authorities) and §7.2 (competence) as context. PDCA: **Plan** (the organization that runs every other module).

**Application:** CALOR Quality System (`QMS/00_Integrated_Application/calor-quality-system.html`) → HT organization. Positions, job descriptions, the organization chart and its approval, people with their qualification and the HTSA 1.1 self-assessment are held there. CALOR's HT organization page (`Organization_Personnel.html`) can be imported into it.

### Where each requirement is met

| Requirement (HTSA 1.1) | Met by | Evidence in the application |
|---|---|---|
| A heat treat position exists and is filled | QP-ORG-01 §6.1; WI-ORG-01 | One position marked "heat treat position"; holder named |
| Dedicated | QP-ORG-01 §6.1 | Position marked dedicated; the holder holds no other position |
| Full-time and on site | QP-ORG-01 §6.1 | Position and person full-time and on site |
| Shown in the organization chart | WI-ORG-02 §1; F-ORG-03 | Reporting line to the chart; chart approved, not changed since, review in date |
| Job description with the qualifications incl. metallurgical and heat treat knowledge | WI-ORG-02 §2; F-ORG-01 | Approved job description stating education, knowledge, minimum years ≥ 5 |
| Evidence of ≥ 5 years heat treat experience, or metallurgical education + experience | WI-ORG-01 §2; F-ORG-02 | Qualification record: dated experience entries, service here, metallurgical education |

### First-cycle implementation checklist

- [ ] Confirm the HTSA 1.1 minimum (5 years) against the controlled CQI-9 copy (Settings).
- [ ] Enter (or import from CALOR) every position with its reporting line; mark the heat treat position, dedicated, and the positions authorized to approve process specifications.
- [ ] Enter the people with their education, experience and training; appoint the holders and deputies.
- [ ] Write and approve the job descriptions, starting with the heat treat position (F-ORG-01).
- [ ] Approve the organization chart (F-ORG-03); post it.
- [ ] Check that the HTSA 1.1 panel reads Satisfactory; file the evidence pack.
- [ ] Approve QP-ORG-01, the two WIs and the formats.
