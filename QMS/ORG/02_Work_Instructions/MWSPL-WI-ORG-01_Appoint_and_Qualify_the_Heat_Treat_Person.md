# MASS WIRE AND STEELS PVT. LTD., BHIWADI

## APPOINT AND QUALIFY THE HEAT TREAT PERSON

| Doc No. | Rev | Issue date | Used by | Prepared | Approved |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MWSPL-WI-ORG-01 | 00 | — | Director, HR executive | | |

### 1. Record the person

| Step | Action | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Application → HT organization → People → "Add a person": name, employee no., full-time, on site, joining date. Tick "Work here since joining is heat treat work" when the job here is heat treatment. | HR executive |
| 1.2 | Education: one line per qualification; tick "Metallurgical" for metallurgy degrees / diplomas and give the duration in years. | HR executive |
| 1.3 | Experience: one line per earlier job with start and end month; tick "Heat treat work" for heat treatment jobs. The certificate or letter number is optional. | HR executive |
| 1.4 | Training: CQI-9, pyrometry, metallurgy courses. | HR executive |

### 2. Check the qualification

| Step | Action | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | The record shows the heat treat experience, the metallurgical education and whether the HTSA 1.1 minimum is met. | Application |
| 2.2 | Below the minimum: the person cannot be the heat treat person; choose another person or record missing experience with evidence. | Director |

### 3. Appoint

| Step | Action | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Open the heat treat position (HT Manager); choose the holder and a deputy; check full-time, on site, dedicated. | Director |
| 3.2 | The holder must not hold another position. | Director |
| 3.3 | Save: the chart is marked changed; approve the new chart revision (WI-ORG-02 §1). | Director |
| 3.4 | Print the HTSA 1.1 evidence pack and file it. | HR executive |
