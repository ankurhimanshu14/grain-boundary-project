## CHANGE A SPECIFICATION: NEW REVISION, IMPLEMENTATION, OBSOLETE

| Doc No. | Rev | Issue date | Parent procedure sections | Prepared | Approved |

**Records:** the new revision with its reason and change review (F-SPEC-02), the snapshot of the earlier revision, the implementation date, the log.

### 1. Start a new revision

| Step | Activity | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Any change to a set value, tolerance, push time, quench / cooling parameter, furnace, loading or requirement needs a new revision. A temporary change at the line is a parameter change on the Daily production page, not a revision; a permanent change is a revision. | HT Manager |
| 1.2 | Open the approved specification and click **New revision**. The page keeps the approved revision in force and starts a draft rev + 1; the change review and implementation fields are cleared. | HT Engineer |
| 1.3 | Make the change; enter the **reason for the change**. | HT Engineer |
| 1.4 | To abandon the change, click **Discard revision**: the revision in force is restored unchanged. | HT Engineer |

### 2. Change review (F-SPEC-02)

| Step | Activity | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Customer requirements checked: state what was checked (drawing, customer specification, PPAP values) and the result. | HT Engineer |
| 2.2 | Customer notification decision: not required / customer notified / customer approval obtained (e.g. PPAP); give the reference when notified or approved. | Quality Manager |
| 2.3 | Tick "control plan and PFMEA reviewed" after checking the CP & PFMEA page (regenerate the control plan rows from the new revision if needed). | Quality Manager |
| 2.4 | Validation: record the trial or first-off result that shows the change works (e.g. trial batch no., hardness range, microstructure). | HT Engineer |

### 3. Approve and implement

| Step | Activity | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Approve the revision as WI-SPEC-01 step 4.3; the gate also requires the reason and the complete change review. | HT Manager (authorized person) |
| 3.2 | Replace the job card / recipe at every approved furnace; withdraw the old card. | Shift supervisor |
| 3.3 | **Record implementation** within 7 days of the approval. After 7 days without it the HTSA 1.6 item "Changes documented with the date implemented and the approver" is not met. | Shift supervisor / HT Engineer |

### 4. Make obsolete

| Step | Activity | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | When a part is no longer heat treated (or is replaced by another specification), click **Make obsolete** and give the reason. | HT Manager |
| 4.2 | Remove its job cards and recipes from the line. | Shift supervisor |
| 4.3 | Only a draft that was never approved can be deleted (**Delete**); approved and obsolete specifications stay in the register. | HT Engineer |
