**Records:** register Excel with the review dates (F-SPEC-04), HTSA 1.6 evidence PDF, KPI format F-SPEC-05.

### 1. Periodic review of each specification (yearly)

| Step | Activity | Responsibility | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Open CALOR → PROCESS → Specifications. The tiles show the reviews due (within 30 days) and overdue (365 days after the approval of the revision in force). | HT Engineer | First working day of the month |
| 1.2 | For each specification due: compare it with the current customer drawing / specification, the hardness and SCADA results of the last year (Hardness, Trend analysis, SCADA review pages) and any customer complaint. | HT Engineer | Before the due date |
| 1.3 | No change needed: the HT Manager confirms it; record the review on F-SPEC-04 (date, by, result). Change needed: start a new revision (WI-SPEC-02); its approval resets the review date. Note: CALOR has no "reviewed, no change" entry for specifications — the register keeps showing the review as overdue until a new revision is approved, so F-SPEC-04 is the record of a review without change (application change requested in the handover note). In the integrated application (pilot) use **Review & HTSA 1.6 → Record review**; it restarts the review date. | HT Manager | Before the due date |

### 2. Active parts without a specification

| Step | Activity | Responsibility | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Read the HTSA 1.6 panel item "Written process specifications for all active processes": it lists every part produced on a furnace in the last 90 days without a specification in force for that furnace. | HT Engineer | Weekly |
| 2.2 | For each one: write and approve the specification (WI-SPEC-01), or add the furnace to the existing one by a new revision (WI-SPEC-02). Until then the part runs only on the HT Manager's written instruction. | HT Manager | Within 7 days |

### 3. HTSA 1.6 self-assessment

| Step | Activity | Responsibility | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Read the HTSA 1.6 panel score: Satisfactory / Not satisfactory / Needs immediate action, and each item (authorization, approval by an authorized person, all active processes, steps with tolerances, available at the line, change review, implementation recorded, periodic review). | HT Manager | Monthly |
| 3.2 | For each item not met, agree the action, owner and date; enter it on F-SPEC-05 (quarterly review). | HT Manager | Monthly |
| 3.3 | Download **HTSA 1.6 evidence (PDF)** and file it with the month's KPIs; it is the evidence for an internal or customer assessment. | Quality Manager | Monthly |
