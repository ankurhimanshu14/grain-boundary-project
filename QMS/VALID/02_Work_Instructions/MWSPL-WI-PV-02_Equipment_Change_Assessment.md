## ASSESSING AN EQUIPMENT CHANGE (RELOCATION, REBUILD, MODIFICATION)

| Doc No. | Rev | Issue date | Owner | Approved |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MWSPL-WI-PV-02 | 00 | — | Maintenance Head | |

### 1. Record the change

Before the equipment returns to production, the Maintenance Head records the change (Process validation → Equipment changes → New change): date, furnace, equipment changed, kind and what was done. Kinds:

| Kind | Revalidation |
|---|---|
| Relocation of process equipment | Always |
| Change of heat treat location | Always (every line) |
| Major rebuild | Always |
| Modification | When assessed major |
| Repair / like-for-like replacement | Normally not; record the reason |

### 2. Assess (HT Manager with the Maintenance Head)

A modification or repair is **major** when it can change any of:

1. the temperature or its uniformity (refractory, burners, zone layout, thermocouple positions, heating capacity);
2. the furnace atmosphere / combustion (burner type, air–gas ratio control, openings, seals);
3. the timing (pusher, extractor, transfer rail, push control);
4. quenching (tank, medium, agitation, transfer, dip) or controlled / fan cooling (fan, air flow, chamber);
5. control and recording (controllers, SCADA, recording, over-temperature protection).

Record the decision with the reason; for a major change the application lists the process lines affected (every line using the equipment changed; a location change affects every line).

### 3. Act

- Major: plan the revalidation of each line affected (WI-PV-01); production waits for the approved validation.
- Not major: the change is closed with the reason; a TUS or SAT may still be needed by the pyrometry rules (Thermocouples / TUS records).
