### 1. Purpose

To show, with objective evidence, that each heat treat process line can yield acceptable product: when the process is first validated, after process equipment is relocated or the heat treat location changes, and after a major rebuild or modification of any equipment of the line (CQI-9 HTSA 1.7).

### 2. Scope

Every process line of furnaces F-01 to F-04: hardening & tempering (F-01 water quench, F-02 oil quench), isothermal annealing (F-01 … F-04) and normalizing (F-02 … F-04), with all the equipment the line uses (hardening chamber, quench tank or controlled cooling chamber, tempering chamber, pusher and extractor, burners and combustion, controls and recording, fan cooling after exit).

### 3. References

- AIAG CQI-9 4th Edition, HTSA 1.7 (controlled copy); Process Tables A and E (pyrometry, monitoring, testing); HTSA 1.6 (process specifications), 1.9 (data review), 3.3 (thermocouples), 3.14 (quench media).
- IATF 16949:2016 §8.5.1.1 (control plan), §8.5.6.1 (control of changes), §8.3.4.4 (product approval).
- MWSPL-WI-PV-01, -02; MWSPL-F-PV-01, -02, -03; MWSPL-QP-SPEC-01; MWSPL-QP-TC-01; MWSPL-QP-QM-01.

### 4. Terms and definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Process line | One heat treat process on one furnace together with every piece of equipment integrated in it, e.g. F-02 hardening & tempering = hardening chamber + oil quench tank + tempering chamber + pusher / extractor + combustion + controls. The application builds the lines from the processes run on each furnace. |
| Process validation | Documented evidence (F-PV-01) that a process line yields acceptable product: robust equipment, process control and monitoring with calibrations, calibrated test equipment, strategic product sampling. |
| Initial validation | The first validation of a process line (new furnace, new process on a furnace, or an existing line without validation records). |
| Relocation / location change | Process equipment moved to another place, or heat treatment moved to another site. Always a revalidation. |
| Major rebuild / modification | A change to the equipment that can affect the temperature, its uniformity, the atmosphere, the timing, quenching or cooling, or the control and recording: e.g. refractory relining, burner or combustion system change, new controls / SCADA, hearth or pusher change, quench tank or agitation change, cooling chamber change. Decided with WI-PV-02; a like-for-like repair or replacement is not major. |

### 6. Procedure

#### 6.1 Process lines

The process lines are the processes run on each furnace (Settings: processes per furnace). Each line lists the equipment it uses (F-PV-03). A change to one piece of equipment affects every line that uses it; a change of location affects every line.

#### 6.2 Initial validation

Every process line is validated before it runs production (WI-PV-01). For lines that ran before this procedure, the commissioning, PPAP and test records may be used when they cover all four elements; otherwise the line is validated now.

#### 6.3 Relocation, rebuild and modification (WI-PV-02)

1. The Maintenance Head records every relocation, location change, rebuild, modification and major repair on F-PV-02 before the equipment returns to production.
2. Relocation and location change always need a revalidation of every line affected.
3. A rebuild or modification is assessed by the HT Manager and the Maintenance Head: major → revalidation; not major → the reason is recorded (e.g. like-for-like replacement).
4. Production does not run on an affected line until the revalidation concludes "validated", except for the trial lots of the validation, which are held until it is approved.
5. The application flags a major change not revalidated within the grace period (Settings) and any production recorded on the furnace after the change before the validation.

#### 6.4 Content of a validation (WI-PV-01, F-PV-01)

- **A. Robust heat treat equipment:** TUS of every chamber of the line over its operating range, over-temperature protection tested, combustion set up, pusher / extractor and timing verified, quench tank (hardening) / controlled cooling (isothermal annealing) / fan cooling (normalizing) verified, equipment qualification record updated.
- **B. Process control, monitoring and calibration:** controllers and recorders calibrated, SAT of the control thermocouples passed, alarms tested, parameters set per the process specification, quench medium within limits (hardening), data review in place.
- **C. Test equipment calibration:** hardness testers calibrated and verified, laboratory equipment calibrated, methods and testers defined.
- **D. Strategic product sampling:** a sampling plan (representative part, lots, trays and positions: front / back, top / bottom, first and last trays) and the trial lots: at least the minimum number of lots and readings per lot (Settings, 〈confirm〉), every reading within the specification, microstructure and decarburization acceptable, capability Cpk at least the target (Settings, 〈confirm〉) or a justified exception.

Every item is answered OK (with the evidence reference and date), Not OK, or Not applicable (with the reason). A Not OK item means the line is not validated.

#### 6.5 Conclusion and approval

The HT Manager concludes "validated" or "not validated" and the report is approved by an authorized person (HT organization). An approved report is locked. A line concluded not validated is corrected and validated again.

#### 6.6 Review

This procedure is reviewed every year (Settings) and after any HTSA finding; a review without change is recorded as a new revision.
