# MASS WIRE AND STEELS PVT. LTD., BHIWADI

## VALID MODULE: HEAT TREAT PROCESS VALIDATION — MASTER LIST

**Basis:** AIAG CQI-9 4th Edition, HTSA question 1.7 ("Has the heat treat process been validated initially and after the process equipment has been relocated, or had a major rebuild or modification?"): to show that each heat treat process can yield acceptable product, the heat treater validates each process initially, after any process equipment is relocated or the heat treat location changes, and after a major rebuild of any equipment. A process line may combine several pieces of equipment that work together in one heat treat process (e.g. hardening, quenching and tempering). Validation consists of robust heat treat equipment, rigorous process control and monitoring requirements and calibrations, appropriate test equipment calibrations and strategic product sampling (wording to confirm against the controlled copy). PDCA: **Plan / Check** (before production on a new or changed process line).

**Application:** CALOR Quality System (`QMS/00_Integrated_Application/calor-quality-system.html`) → Process validation. The procedure approval, the process lines, the equipment change assessments, the validation records (four elements and product sampling trials), the validation status of each line and the HTSA 1.7 self-assessment are held there.

### Where each requirement is met

| Requirement (HTSA 1.7) | Met by | Evidence in the application |
|---|---|---|
| Initial validation of each process | QP-PV-01 §6.2; WI-PV-01 | Process lines: every line (furnace × process) with an approved validation that concludes "validated" |
| After relocation of process equipment or a change of heat treat location | QP-PV-01 §6.3; WI-PV-02 | Equipment changes of kind relocation / location change: always need a revalidation of every line they affect |
| After a major rebuild or modification | QP-PV-01 §6.3; WI-PV-02; F-PV-02 | Equipment changes assessed (major / not major with the reason); every major change revalidated |
| A process line = the equipment integrated in one process (hardening, quench, tempering) | QP-PV-01 §4; F-PV-03 | Process lines with their equipment; a change affects every line using that equipment |
| Robust heat treat equipment | WI-PV-01 §3.1; F-PV-01 part A | TUS, over-temperature protection, combustion, pusher, quench / controlled cooling / fan cooling, equipment record |
| Process control, monitoring and calibration | WI-PV-01 §3.2; F-PV-01 part B | Controller / recorder calibration, SAT, alarms, parameters per specification, quench medium, data review |
| Test equipment calibration | WI-PV-01 §3.3; F-PV-01 part C | Hardness testers verified, laboratory equipment calibrated, methods and testers defined |
| Strategic product sampling | WI-PV-01 §3.4; F-PV-01 part D | Sampling plan, trial lots with readings, minimum lots and readings, capability |

### First-cycle implementation checklist

- [ ] Approve QP-PV-01, the two WIs and the formats; record the approval in the application (Procedure & lines).
- [ ] Check the processes run on each furnace (Settings) so that the process lines are right.
- [ ] Record the initial validation of every process line (existing lines: from the commissioning / PPAP records; a line without records is validated now).
- [ ] Record every relocation, rebuild or modification since the last validation and assess it (WI-PV-02).
- [ ] Revalidate every line with a major change; check that HTSA 1.7 reads Satisfactory; file the evidence.
