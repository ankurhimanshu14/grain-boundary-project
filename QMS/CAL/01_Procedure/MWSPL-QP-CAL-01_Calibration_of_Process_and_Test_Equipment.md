### 1. Purpose

To keep every calibration, verification and certification of the heat treat process and test equipment current at the intervals of the applicable Process Tables, Sections 1.0 and 2.0, to control the offsets applied to the instrumentation (P3.2.3) and to identify calibrated equipment by labels (P3.2.5.1) and reports (P3.2.5.2) (CQI-9 HTSA 3.2).

### 2. Scope

The process and test equipment of furnaces F-01 to F-04 and the laboratory: zone temperature controllers, SCADA recording channels, over-temperature controllers, process timers (push cycle, controlled cooling, quench dip), quench medium temperature indicators, combustion gauges, field test instruments for SAT / TUS, non-contact thermometers, hardness testers and test blocks, the microscope. Test thermocouples and their instruments are controlled by MWSPL-QP-TC-01 and counted in the register. Measuring equipment outside the heat treat process follows the IATF 16949 calibration system 〈reference the plant's MSA / calibration procedure〉.

### 4. Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Calibration | Comparison with a traceable reference standard at defined points, recording the values as found and, after any adjustment, as left. |
| Verification | A check against a reference without adjustment (e.g. a timer against a stop watch, a hardness tester on certified blocks). |
| Certification | A certificate of the supplier or a laboratory for an item that is not adjusted (e.g. a hardness test block). |
| Offset (bias) | A fixed value entered into a controller, recorder or SCADA channel that shifts the displayed or controlled value, to adjust the instrumentation calibration. |
| As found / as left | The reading before / after the adjustment; as found out of tolerance triggers the product assessment. |

### 6. Procedure

#### 6.1 Register (F-CAL-01)
Every item is registered with its tag, location, type (calibration / verification / certification), range of use, accuracy requirement, interval and the Process Table item. The interval may be shorter than, but not longer than, the frequency of the Process Table for its kind 〈frequencies to confirm per Process Table A / E〉.

#### 6.2 Schedule
The application gives the due date of each item (latest valid record + interval, or the due date on the report). Items due soon are planned; an overdue item is not used until calibrated. A spare item is calibrated before it is put into use.

#### 6.3 Calibration
Per WI-CAL-01: calibrated at points covering the range of use with a reference standard that is calibrated and traceable to national / international standards; errors as found and as left are recorded against the accuracy requirement. External laboratories are accredited for the scope (ISO/IEC 17025, e.g. NABL).

#### 6.4 Non-contact thermometry
Infrared and optical pyrometers are calibrated at the interval of the Process Table against a blackbody or a calibrated contact reference at the temperatures of use; the emissivity setting used is recorded and kept on the device.

#### 6.5 Offsets (P3.2.3)
Offsets are applied only per WI-CAL-02, which names the people authorized to approve them and how the approval is documented.

#### 6.6 Labels and reports
Each calibrated item carries a label per WI-CAL-01 §6 (P3.2.5.1) and has a report per WI-CAL-01 §7 (P3.2.5.2), reviewed and accepted within the period in Settings.

#### 6.7 Found out of tolerance
The Quality Manager assesses the product processed since the last calibration in tolerance (the window shown by the application), decides containment and disposition; the cause and action are recorded with owner and date. Equipment out of tolerance as left is taken out of service until calibrated.
