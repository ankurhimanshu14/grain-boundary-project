# MASS WIRE AND STEELS PVT. LTD., BHIWADI

## CALIBRATION, LABELS AND REPORTS

| Doc No. | Rev | Owner | Approved |
|---|---|---|---|
| MWSPL-WI-CAL-01 | 00 | Quality Manager | |

### 1. Before the calibration
Check the due list (Calibration → Register & schedule). Check that the reference standard is in date on the calibration day and note its certificate and traceability.

### 2. Points
Record at least three points covering the range of use (low, middle, high); for a controller or recorder, simulate the thermocouple input (type K) at the input terminals, with cold-junction compensation active.

### 3. As found and as left
Enter the reading at each point before any adjustment (as found). If adjusted, enter the reading after the adjustment (as left); leave as left empty when nothing was adjusted. The application compares each error with the accuracy (± value or ± % of reading, the greater) 〈confirm against P3.2〉.

### 4. Temperature instrumentation
Controllers, SCADA channels and over-temperature controllers: simulated input at three points; record the adjustment (calibrated) or, only per WI-CAL-02, an offset.

### 5. Non-contact thermometers
Calibrate against a blackbody source or a calibrated contact reference at the temperatures of use; record the emissivity setting used.

### 6. Label (P3.2.5.1, F-CAL-04)
The label at the equipment shows: equipment identification, calibration date, next due date, calibrated by, and any limitation of use 〈confirm the elements against P3.2.5.1〉. Check the label after fitting it and tick the elements in the record ("Label checked by").

### 7. Report (P3.2.5.2, F-CAL-02)
The report shows: equipment identification and serial no., date and next due, calibrated by (laboratory and accreditation when external), report no., method, reference standard with certificate, calibration due and traceability, the points as found and as left, the accuracy requirement, the adjustment or offset applied, the statement on an offset in use, the emissivity for non-contact devices, signatures 〈confirm the elements against P3.2.5.2〉. The Quality Manager (or a person in Settings / the HT organization) reviews and accepts it.

### 8. Found out of tolerance
Do not release product made since the last good calibration until the Quality Manager has assessed it (Calibration → Actions).
