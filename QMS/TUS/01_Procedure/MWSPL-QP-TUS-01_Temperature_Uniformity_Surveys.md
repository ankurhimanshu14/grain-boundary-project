### 1. Purpose

To prove, by temperature uniformity surveys (TUS) performed and reported as required by the applicable Process Tables Section 2.0 and the pyrometry section P3.4, that every chamber holds its qualified temperature ranges within the tolerance (CQI-9 HTSA 3.4).

### 2. Scope

The hardening and tempering chambers of furnaces F-01 to F-04 (direct gas-fired pusher furnaces, 3 zones each). The controlled cooling chamber is outside the scope of TUS unless a Process Table or customer requires it 〈confirm〉.

### 3. References

- AIAG CQI-9 4th Edition: HTSA 3.4; P3.4 (P3.4.1.2 changes, P3.4.5.1 tolerance and soak, P3.4.7 reporting, P3.4.8 alternate methods); Process Tables A and E, Section 2.0 (controlled copy).
- MWSPL-QP-TC-01 (thermocouples, SAT), MWSPL-QP-PV-01 (process validation), MWSPL-QP-SPEC-01 (process specifications).
- MWSPL-WI-TUS-01, -02; MWSPL-F-TUS-01, -02, -03.

### 4. Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Qualified range | The temperature span a chamber is used at, with its uniformity tolerance and survey interval from the Process Table. A multi-role chamber (e.g. the tempering chamber that austenitizes for normalizing) is qualified over its whole span. |
| Range end | A survey counts for an end when its set value is within the end tolerance (Settings) of the lower or upper limit. A range is qualified when both ends have a passing survey within the interval. |
| Stabilisation | The first reading from which every survey thermocouple is within the tolerance. |
| Soak time | The time at temperature after stabilisation over which the pass / fail is judged, defined by the internal process specification (Settings). |
| Alternate method | Any survey method other than survey thermocouples placed in the qualified work zone (P3.4.8). |

### 6. Procedure

#### 6.1 Qualified ranges (F-TUS-01)
Each chamber has its qualified range(s) with tolerance and survey interval taken from the applicable Process Table Section 2.0 and the furnace class; the reference is recorded with the range.

#### 6.2 Surveys
Each range is surveyed at its lower and upper end within the interval (and initially, after a change per §6.6, and after a failed survey). WI-TUS-01 describes the method; the application keeps the readings.

#### 6.3 Method
The standard method places calibrated survey thermocouples in the qualified work zone at the locations of the plan (at least the number in Settings 〈confirm against P3.4〉). An alternate method (P3.4.8) is used only with a documented method reference, the justification and the approval of the HT Manager (and the customer where required).

#### 6.4 Tolerance and soak
- Every reading of every survey thermocouple, corrected, must stay at or below set value + tolerance at all times, also during heat-up and before stabilisation. An exception is allowed only for a process cycle with several process temperatures (P3.4.5.1); it is recorded on the survey with the reason.
- After stabilisation the readings are judged against set value ± tolerance over the soak time defined in the internal process specification (Settings: soak time and its reference). A survey that does not cover the soak time is incomplete.

#### 6.5 Report and approval (P3.4.7, F-TUS-02)
The report contains the identification of furnace and chamber, date and times, set value and qualified range, tolerance, method, the test instrument and survey thermocouples with calibration and correction factors, the thermocouple locations, all readings with times, stabilisation, the hottest and coldest location, the result, deviations and the approval. The application does not let a report be approved without them.

#### 6.6 Changes that alter uniformity (P3.4.1.2, F-TUS-03)
Every repair, rebuild, burner or combustion change, refractory work, control thermocouple relocation, controller tuning or other change that can alter the uniformity of a chamber is recorded with the decision whether a re-survey is needed (and why not). Production on the range waits for the passing re-survey when one is required. Equipment changes recorded in Process validation for the chamber are flagged until documented here.

#### 6.7 Failed survey
Product in the chamber since the last passing survey at that set value is assessed (Quality Manager); cause, action, owner and date are recorded; the range is closed only with a passing re-survey.

### 7. Records
F-TUS-01, -02, -03 in the application; retention 〈confirm: life of the furnace + 1 year or per customer〉.
