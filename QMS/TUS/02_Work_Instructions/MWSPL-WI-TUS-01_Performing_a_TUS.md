# MASS WIRE AND STEELS PVT. LTD., BHIWADI

## PERFORMING A TEMPERATURE UNIFORMITY SURVEY

| Doc No. | Rev | Owner | Approved |
|---|---|---|---|
| MWSPL-WI-TUS-01 | 00 | HT Manager | |

| Step | Action | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Check the schedule (Uniformity (TUS) → Schedule): range end, set value. | HT Manager |
| 2 | Check the test instrument and the survey thermocouples: calibration in date on the survey day, correction factors at the set value. | Metrology |
| 3 | Place the survey thermocouples at the planned locations (zone 1–3, right / centre / left; front and back of the work zone). Note any change of location. | Survey team |
| 4 | Choose the method: standard (thermocouples in the work zone) or, only with an approved reference, an alternate method (P3.4.8). | HT Manager |
| 5 | Record from the start of heat-up at the reading interval (Settings) until the soak time after stabilisation is covered. | Survey team |
| 6 | Enter the record: New survey → identification, instrument and thermocouples with corrections and locations, paste the readings (a leading time column is allowed). | Survey team |
| 7 | Check the result on screen: overshoot above set value + tolerance at any time, stabilisation, soak covered, hottest / coldest. | Survey team |
