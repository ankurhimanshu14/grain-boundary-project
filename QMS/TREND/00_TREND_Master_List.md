# MASS WIRE AND STEELS PVT. LTD., BHIWADI

## TREND MODULE: DATA ANALYSIS OVER TIME — MASTER LIST

**Basis:** AIAG CQI-9 4th Edition, HTSA question 1.8 ("Does the heat treater collect and analyze data over time, and react to this data?"). The organization shall have a system to collect, analyze and react to product or process data over time. Methods of analysis shall include ongoing trend or historical data analysis of product characteristics or process parameters. The organization determines which parameters are included. Examples: product property trend charts (e.g. tensile strength), scrap trends, variation in process parameter recordings (e.g. temperature). Wording to confirm against the controlled copy. IATF 16949 §9.1.3 (analysis and evaluation) and §10.3 (continual improvement) as context. PDCA: **Check** (the data over time) leading to **Act** (the reaction).

**Application:** CALOR Quality System (`QMS/00_Integrated_Application/calor-quality-system.html`) → Trend analysis. The analysis plan (the parameters determined), the data, the charts with their signals, the chart reviews and their actions, and the HTSA 1.8 self-assessment are held there.

### Where each requirement is met

| Requirement (HTSA 1.8) | Met by | Evidence in the application |
|---|---|---|
| A system to collect, analyze and react to data over time | QP-TREND-01 | Analysis plan approved (revision, approved by, date), periodic plan review |
| The organization determines the parameters | WI-TREND-01; F-TREND-01 | Parameters in the plan: product characteristics and process parameters, each with its source, limits, review interval, owner and reaction rule |
| Data collected over time | QP-TREND-01 §6.2 | Points per parameter from the SCADA review, the SAT records, CALOR's registers or entered data |
| Ongoing trend / historical analysis | QP-TREND-01 §6.3 | Individuals chart, control limits, run rules, Cpk; charts reviewed at their interval |
| Reaction to the data | WI-TREND-02; F-TREND-02 | Chart reviews: decision (action / watch / no action with a reason), action, owner, due, done, verified in the data |

### First-cycle implementation checklist

- [ ] Approve QP-TREND-01, the two WIs and the formats.
- [ ] Determine the parameters (WI-TREND-01): at least one product characteristic and one process parameter, and a process parameter for every furnace in use.
- [ ] Approve the analysis plan in the application (Analysis plan → Approve).
- [ ] Review every chart at its interval (WI-TREND-02); act on signals.
- [ ] Check that HTSA 1.8 reads Satisfactory; file the evidence (HTSA 1.8 → Evidence).
