### 1. When

At the parameter's review interval, and at once when a point is outside the specification. The dashboard lists the charts due and the signals waiting for a review.

### 2. Read the chart

| Look for | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Point outside the specification | Product may be nonconforming: hold the lot, inform the HT Manager, follow the nonconforming product procedure. |
| Point beyond the control limits | Something changed: find the cause (furnace, thermocouple, material heat, quench). |
| 8 points on one side of the mean | Shift: compare with set value changes, thermocouple replacements, a new material supplier. |
| 6 points rising or falling | Trend: drift (thermocouple ageing, quench oil ageing, tool wear). |
| 2 of 3 points beyond 2σ | Early warning of a shift. |
| Cpk below 1.33 | Capability: the process uses too much of the tolerance. |

### 3. Record the review (F-TREND-02)

| Step | Action | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | "Review" on the parameter: date, reviewed by, what the data shows. | Owner |
| 3.2 | Decision: **Action** (what, owner, due date), **Watch** (reason) or **No action needed** (reason). | Owner |
| 3.3 | When the action is done: done date and the verification seen in the later data. | Action owner |

A signal stays "to review" until a review dated on or after it is recorded.
