### 1. Purpose

To keep every quenching medium within the tolerances that give the specified hardness without cracks or distortion: analyze it periodically for the characteristics of the applicable CQI-9 Process Table, Section 5.0, judge each result against tolerances set by the supplier or the heat treater, review and document the results, and react when a result is out of limits (CQI-9 HTSA 3.14).

### 2. Scope

The quench tanks of the hardening and tempering line: F-01 (water) and F-02 (oil). F-03 and F-04 run isothermal annealing and normalizing only and have no quench tank. A polymer quenchant, if introduced, follows this procedure.

### 3. References

- AIAG CQI-9 4th Edition, HTSA 3.14; Process Table A, Section 5.0 (controlled copy).
- The quenchant supplier's data sheets and recommended limits.
- MWSPL-WI-QM-01, -02; MWSPL-F-QM-01, -02; nonconforming product procedure.

### 4. Responsibility

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HT Manager | Owns this procedure; approves the test plan and its tolerances; reviews the laboratory results; decides on product at risk |
| Furnace Supervisor | Daily in-house checks (temperature, level, agitation, contamination); stops quenching when a check fails |
| Laboratory Supervisor | Samples for the laboratory, in-house laboratory tests, records and first review of the results |

### 5. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Test plan | The list of characteristics per quenchant, with method, frequency and tolerances (F-QM-01). |
| Source of a tolerance | The quench medium supplier (data sheet, recommendation) or the heat treater (own limit, justified). |
| Review | A qualified person checks each result against its tolerance and signs the record with the date. |

### 6. Procedure

#### 6.1 Test plan

The test plan names, per quenchant, every characteristic required by the applicable Process Table Section 5.0 (for oil e.g. cooling curve, water content, viscosity, flash point, acid number, sludge; for water / brine e.g. temperature, contamination, salt concentration), the test method, the frequency and the tolerance with its source and reference. The HT Manager approves the plan; any change is approved again. The plan is reviewed every 365 days and when the quenchant or its supplier changes.

#### 6.2 Sampling and analysis

Daily checks are done at the tank by the shift (WI-QM-01). Laboratory analyses are done by the in-house laboratory or by the supplier / an outside laboratory on a representative sample taken as WI-QM-01 states; the report number is recorded.

#### 6.3 Review

Each result is judged against its tolerance (out of limits, close to a limit, within limits) and reviewed and signed by the Laboratory Supervisor or the HT Manager within 2 working days (site rule). Laboratory reports are reviewed by the HT Manager.

#### 6.4 Reaction

A result out of limits: stop quenching or hold the product of the window since the last passing result, find the cause, correct the quenchant, assess the product impact, and re-test until the characteristic is back within its tolerance (WI-QM-02 §3).

### 7. Records

| Record | Kept as | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| Test plan and its approval | F-QM-01 (application) | Current + superseded revisions |
| Test records, laboratory reports, reviews | F-QM-02 (application) + the laboratory report | 3 years minimum, or as the customer requires |
| Reaction records | F-QM-02 | 3 years |
