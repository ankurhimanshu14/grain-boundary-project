# MASS WIRE AND STEELS PVT. LTD., BHIWADI

## QUENCH MODULE: QUENCH MEDIA ANALYSIS — MASTER LIST

**Basis:** AIAG CQI-9 4th Edition, HTSA question 3.14 ("Is the quenching medium analyzed?"): the heat treater periodically has the quenching medium analyzed for specific quenching characteristics, e.g. cooling curve, water content, salt concentration, as specified in the applicable Process Tables, Section 5.0; the tolerances of the quench medium characteristics are specified by the quench medium supplier or the heat treater; test results are reviewed for conformance and documented by the heat treater. Wording to confirm against the controlled copy. Process Table A (hardening & tempering) applies to F-01 (water) and F-02 (oil); F-03 and F-04 have no quench tank. PDCA: **Plan** (test plan) → **Do** (sampling, analysis) → **Check** (review) → **Act** (reaction).

**Application:** CALOR Quality System (`QMS/00_Integrated_Application/calor-quality-system.html`) → Quench media.

### Where each requirement is met

| Requirement (HTSA 3.14) | Met by | Evidence in the application |
|---|---|---|
| Quench medium analyzed periodically for the characteristics of Process Table Section 5.0 | QP-QM-01 §6.1–6.2; F-QM-01 | Approved test plan per quenchant (parameters, method, frequency); schedule per tank (due, overdue, never tested) |
| Tolerances specified by the supplier or the heat treater | QP-QM-01 §6.1; F-QM-01 | Limits of every judged parameter with their source (supplier data sheet / heat treater) and reference |
| Results reviewed for conformance and documented | WI-QM-02; F-QM-02 | Test records with every value judged against its limits, laboratory report reference, reviewer and date |
| Reaction to results out of limits (good practice, linked to 3.14) | WI-QM-02 §3 | Reaction plan: containment, cause, action, product impact, owner, date and a passing re-test |

### First-cycle implementation checklist

- [ ] Approve QP-QM-01, the WIs and the formats.
- [ ] Register the quench tanks (F-01 water, F-02 oil) with quenchant, supplier and charge date.
- [ ] Confirm the test plan against Process Table A Section 5.0 and the supplier's data sheets; record the source of each tolerance; approve the plan.
- [ ] Record the daily checks and the laboratory analyses; review each result.
- [ ] Check that HTSA 3.14 reads Satisfactory; file the evidence.
