### 1. Recording a result

Application → Quench media → Record a test: tank, date, time, tested by, laboratory and report number, the values. Each value is judged at once against its tolerance: **Out of limits**, **Close to a limit** (within 10 % of the band, setting), **Within limits**, or **Recorded** (no tolerance set).

### 2. Review

| Step | Action | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Check every value against the tolerance and the method; for a laboratory report check the sample identity and the date. | Laboratory Supervisor |
| 2.2 | Enter "Reviewed by" and the date, with remarks (trend, close to a limit). | Laboratory Supervisor / HT Manager |
| 2.3 | Laboratory analyses (cooling curve, viscosity, acid number, sludge, flash point) are reviewed by the HT Manager. | HT Manager |

### 3. Reaction to a result out of limits

| Step | Action | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Containment: stop quenching, or hold the product quenched since the last passing result. | Furnace Supervisor / HT Manager |
| 3.2 | Root cause (water ingress from a cooler, contamination, degradation, temperature control, agitation). | Maintenance Head |
| 3.3 | Corrective action (heat out water, filter, top up, recharge, repair). | Maintenance Head |
| 3.4 | Product impact: hardness / crack checks of the lots of the window; decision on the product. | HT Manager, Quality Manager |
| 3.5 | Re-test until every failed characteristic is back within tolerance; the record closes only then. | Laboratory Supervisor |
