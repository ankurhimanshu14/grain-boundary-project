### 1. Daily checks (each shift or as the test plan states)

| Check | Oil tank (F-02) | Water tank (F-01) | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| Temperature | Tank indicator; once a week against a calibrated thermometer | Same | Furnace Supervisor |
| Level within the marks | Visual | Visual | Furnace Supervisor |
| Agitation / circulation running | Visual: agitator and pump | Same | Furnace Supervisor |
| Contamination (water, oil film, scale, foam) | Crackle test for water when in doubt | Visual | Furnace Supervisor |

A check out of limits: stop loading for quench, inform the HT Manager, record it (Application → Quench media → Record a test).

### 2. Sampling for the laboratory

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 2.1 | Agitation running for at least 15 minutes before sampling, quenchant at working temperature. |
| 2.2 | Take the sample from mid-depth, away from the inlet and the surface, into a clean, dry bottle supplied by the laboratory (oil: 1 litre). |
| 2.3 | Label: tank, furnace, quenchant, date, time, sampled by. |
| 2.4 | Send on the same day; record the dispatch and, later, the report number with the results. |
