Method
  • CQI-9 expects documented start-up, shutdown and emergency procedures, alarms that are checked and answered, and a reaction for product in the furnace when the process is interrupted. This log is the record of each start-up, shutdown and alarm against those procedures; the question numbers and process-table items it gives evidence for are to be confirmed against the controlled CQI-9 copy.
  • A start-up is complete when the critical checklist items are OK, the times are recorded (heating started, all zones within tolerance, first tray) and the shift supervisor has verified it. The first tray must wait the soak time after all zones are within tolerance; inside CALOR the first hardness test of the furnace after the first tray is looked up on the Hardness page.
  • An alarm record gives the response time (alarm → acknowledged) against the matrix, the duration (alarm → cleared) and the product decision. A reaction plan (containment, disposition, cause, action, owner, date) is needed when product was or may have been affected, the response was late, or the same alarm repeats in the furnace; the record closes only when it is complete and reviewed.
  • Inside CALOR the batches in the furnace during an alarm come from the Daily production page, and the SCADA review takes the furnace start and stop from this log when its own start / stop fields are empty.
