Every SAT is one cycle that moves through the four stages. A cycle cannot advance until the stage's required entries (*) are complete. Click a card to work on it.

  • Plan — schedule the SAT, choose the calibrated test instrument and test thermocouple.
  • Do — run the test at the operating set value and record readings and corrections.
  • Check — SAT difference vs tolerance, drift since the last SAT, validity of the test.
  • Act — correct and re-test failures, assess product at risk, set the next interval.

Method
  • Each SAT records a series of furnace and test reading pairs (default 30 readings, one per minute, over 30 minutes). The SAT difference is the mean of the per-reading differences and is judged against the tolerance; individual readings outside tolerance move a pass into the warning band, and a furnace range above the stability limit is flagged.
  • SAT per CQI-9: a calibrated test thermocouple is placed next to the furnace control/recording thermocouple tip and both readings are compared at the normal operating set value with the furnace stable.
  • Default tolerance ±5.5 °C (±10 °F) and quarterly frequency (90 days); both can be changed in Settings. Readings within the warning band (default 70 % of tolerance) pass but need preventive action.
  • A test is invalid when the test instrument calibration had expired on the test day or the furnace was not stable; it must be repeated (Do).
  • Thermocouples are registered per furnace (F-01 … F-04) and chamber (hardening, tempering). A replacement record updates the installed element; the thermocouple is then due for a verification SAT on or after the replacement date.
  • A failed SAT closes only after root cause, corrective action, product-impact assessment and a passing re-SAT.
