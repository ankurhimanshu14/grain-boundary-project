Loading standards

How each part is loaded: tray or fixture, pieces per tray, arrangement, orientation, separators, load height and cleanliness. The standard in force is the work instruction at the loading station; it is checked against the process specification (pieces per tray) and the tray's maximum load.

Load checks

Verification of the load build when a batch is loaded: pattern, orientation, pieces per tray, separators, cleanliness of the parts, condition of the trays used and identification. A check that finds the load not as the standard needs a correction before the trays are pushed and a reaction plan.

Method
  • CQI-9 expects parts to be loaded to a defined load build (fixture or tray, quantity, arrangement and orientation) so that every part sees the same heating, quench and cooling, and trays, baskets and fixtures to be kept in a condition that does not distort or contaminate the parts. The question numbers and process-table items this page gives evidence for are to be confirmed against the controlled CQI-9 copy.
  • A loading standard is approved like a controlled document (revision, prepared and approved by, reason for a change). Its pieces per tray must match the process specification in force, and the net load (pieces × part weight) must stay within the tray's maximum load.
  • A load check verifies the first trays of each batch against the standard in force. Critical items not OK, or more pieces than the standard, make the check not OK: the load is corrected before the trays are pushed and a reaction plan (containment, cause, action, owner, date) closes it. Inside CALOR the batches of the Daily production page show which batches have a load check.
  • A tray or fixture is inspected at the set interval; a crack, broken weld, missing bars, distortion beyond the limit or heavy build-up takes it out of use (repair or scrap). Its life is counted in months in service and, inside CALOR, in cycles estimated from the trays pushed on its furnace (trays loaded ÷ trays of the same kind in use on the furnace).
