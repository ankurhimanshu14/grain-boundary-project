Hardness and metallurgical testing

How a test moves through PDCA

  • Plan — the requirements and sampling come from the process specification in force for the part (hardness, core hardness, decarburization, microstructure, grain size).
  • Do — test the samples of the batch on a calibrated, verified tester and record every reading.
  • Check — every reading is judged against the limits; the test is invalid when the tester's calibration had expired.
  • Act — a nonconforming batch needs containment, root cause, correction, product-impact assessment and a disposition; it is accepted only after a passing re-test.

Test equipment

Verification tolerance: the allowed difference between the mean of the readings on the test block and the block's certified value, in the block's scale. Confirm it against the test standard (e.g. ISO 6508 / ASTM E18 for Rockwell, ISO 6506 / ASTM E10 for Brinell) and the block certificate.

Method
  • CQI-9 requires in-process and final tests to the part's requirements, on calibrated equipment that is verified before use, with the results recorded per batch and nonconforming product contained. Confirm the test methods, sampling and limits against the controlled CQI-9 copy, the customer's requirements and the process specification.
  • Surface hardness: every reading is judged against the specified minimum and maximum; a reading within the warning margin (default 10 % of the band) passes but is flagged. Core hardness is judged against its own limits when they are given.
  • Decarburization: the measured depth (total, mm) must not exceed the limit of the specification. The furnaces are direct-fired without a protective atmosphere, so it is checked on every tested batch.
  • Microstructure is judged OK / not OK against the specified structure; grain size is recorded.
  • A test is invalid when the tester's calibration had expired on the test day; a missing daily verification is reported.
