HTSA 1.1 · Dedicated and qualified heat treat person on site

The requirement, the evidence this page holds for it and the resulting assessment. The scoring follows the HTSA categories; confirm the wording and the scoring rules against the controlled CQI-9 copy.

1.1 Is there a dedicated and qualified heat treat person on-site?To ensure readily available expertise the following requirements shall be met:
  • There shall be a dedicated and qualified full-time heat treat person on site.
  • The position shall be reflected in the organization chart.
  • A job description shall exist identifying the qualifications for the positions including appropriate metallurgical and heat treat knowledge for the individuals.
  • Evidence shall be available regarding the qualifications with a minimum of 5 years experience in heat treat operations or as a combination of a minimum of 5 years of formal metallurgical education and heat treat experience.

Designated heat treat person

The holder of the position marked as the heat treat position, and the deputy who covers absences. Qualification counts only experience and education with evidence on file.

Organization chart

Positions and job descriptions

Every position in the chart with its holder, deputy and job description. The job description states the purpose, responsibilities and authority of the position and the qualifications it needs, including the metallurgical and heat treat knowledge and the years of experience.

People and qualification evidence

Education, experience and training of each person with the evidence on file (certificate, experience letter, appointment letter). Heat treat experience is counted from dated entries marked as heat treat work; overlapping periods count once.

Data

Method
  • Dedicated: heat treatment is the main duty of the position (ticked on the position) and its holder holds no other position in the chart. Full-time and on site are checked on both the position and the person's employment.
  • Organization chart: built from the positions (“reports to”). It is approved with a revision and date; any change to a position, holder or reporting line after the approval asks for a new approval. A periodic re-approval is due after the review interval.
  • Job description: a controlled document per position (draft → approved, new revision with a reason). For the heat treat position it must state the education, the metallurgical and heat treat knowledge and a minimum experience of at least the HTSA minimum.
  • Process specifications (HTSA 1.6): the holder and the deputy of the heat treat position, and of every position marked “authorized to establish and approve process specifications”, are the people the Specifications page accepts as approvers.
  • Qualification: heat treat experience = the dated experience entries marked as heat treat work with evidence (overlaps counted once, an open entry runs to today); formal metallurgical education = the duration of metallurgy / materials programmes with evidence. Qualified when the heat treat experience reaches the minimum, or when the heat treat experience plus the metallurgical education together reach it (both present).
