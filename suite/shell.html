CALOR data

Everything you enter is saved in this browser on this computer. All pages share it, and so do the separate page files (production, specifications, SCADA review, quench media, hardness, control plan, SAT, TUS, equipment) when they are opened from this computer in the same browser.

To move the data to another computer or browser, or to keep a copy, download a backup and restore it there.

Data per tab

Reset settings puts the tab's settings back to the defaults and keeps its records. Delete data deletes everything that tab has saved (records and settings); the other tabs are not touched.

Clear all data (every tab)

Deletes everything CALOR has saved in this browser: production reports, process specifications, SAT register and cycles, temperature uniformity surveys, equipment qualification records, control plans and PFMEAs, thermocouple replacements, quench media tests, hardness and metallurgical tests, SCADA reviews and reaction plans, SCADA history, links and settings. The separate page files (production, specifications, SCADA review, quench media, hardness, control plan, SAT, TUS, equipment) opened in this browser use the same data, so they are cleared too. Download a backup first if you may need any of it.
