Method
  • CQI-9 4th Edition: hardening & tempering (with the quench) is covered by Process Table A; normalizing and isothermal annealing by Process Table E. Confirm the table letters and every numeric requirement against the controlled copy of CQI-9 and the customer-specific requirements.
  • A specification holds the part, steel grade, process, the furnaces it is approved for, the load, the set value and tolerance of every zone, the push time, the quench or controlled-cooling parameters, the quality requirements with sampling, and the approval.
  • Time per zone = tray positions of the zone × push time. Time through the line per tray = (positions of both chambers) × push time: a tray is taken out of the hardening chamber and out of the tempering chamber at a push, so the time between the chambers shortens the last tempering zone instead of adding to the total. Hardening chamber 13 positions (5 + 4 + 4), tempering chamber 12 (4 + 4 + 4). Between the chambers: hardening & tempering = quench delay + dip time + transfer; isothermal annealing = the controlled cooling cycle: at each push the extractor takes the 13th tray out of the hardening chamber (and the 26th out of the tempering chamber); the tray goes on the rail into the controlled cooling chamber, air is blown for the cooling time, then it is pushed into the tempering chamber. Cooling time + transfers (setting) must end before the next push, because the controlled cooling chamber holds one tray; normalizing = transfer.
  • The line checks (hardening chamber above 800 °C for hardening and isothermal annealing, 700–750 °C preheat for normalizing, tempering below the austenitizing set value) follow this line's practice; they are warnings, not CQI-9 requirements.
  • The furnaces are direct-fired without a protective atmosphere, so scale and surface decarburization are expected: specify the decarburization limit and check it.
